Climate Impact Documentation

Climate Impact Documentation is the Foresee Center's front-facing platform: a multimedia storytelling and communication effort that makes the impacts of a changing climate visible and legible to a wider audience. It presents the 3D models, maps, and storymaps produced by the Foresee Explorers Lab, turning field data from places like Mustang District, Nepal into narratives anyone can explore. The interactive map on this site is one of its projects.
