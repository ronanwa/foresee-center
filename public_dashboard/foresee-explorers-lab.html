Foresee Explorers Lab

The Foresee Explorers Lab is the Foresee Center's methods lab, working at the intersection of computer science and electrical engineering. It builds the tools — sensing systems, capture software, and AI pipelines — and carries out the fieldwork to gather 3D scans, sensor data, and imagery from remote and rapidly changing environments. Its output feeds Climate Impact Documentation, where it is shaped into public-facing stories.
