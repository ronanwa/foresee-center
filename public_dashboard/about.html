About

The Foresee Center, also known as the Center for Climate, Conflict, and Change, is an interdisciplinary research group of engineers and explorers leveraging AI for climate adaptation, environmental conservation, and heritage preservation. We foresee a wave of consequences due to climate change, and our mission is to document, monitor, and mitigate as these changes come.
